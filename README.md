**🏦 Enterprise Cyber Risk & Compliance Dashboard
📌 Project Overview**
This project presents an Enterprise Cyber Risk & Compliance Dashboard developed in Power BI.
The objective of this dashboard is to simulate how a bank monitors cybersecurity exposure, regulatory compliance posture, and control effectiveness in a structured and executive-ready format.
The dashboard consolidates cyber incidents, control assessments, and vulnerability data into a single-page risk view designed for leadership, audit committees, and regulatory reporting.

**🎯 Business Objective**
Modern financial institutions operate under strict regulatory frameworks and cybersecurity standards. Leadership requires clear visibility into:
Overall cyber risk exposure
Regulatory non-compliance areas
Control environment maturity
Open high-severity incidents
Critical vulnerability exposure
Risk concentration across business units
This dashboard transforms operational risk data into meaningful insights to support strategic decision-making and proactive risk mitigation.

**🛠 Tools & Technologies**
Power BI
Data modeling and relational schema design
DAX measures for KPI calculations
Conditional formatting and executive-level visual design

**📊 Key KPIs Implemented**
The dashboard includes the following core indicators:
Average CVSS Risk Score – Measures overall vulnerability severity.
Control Effectiveness Rate – Percentage of controls assessed as effective.
Non-Compliant Controls (Open) – Controls failing regulatory standards.
Open High-Severity Incidents – Unresolved High and Critical incidents.
Open Critical Vulnerabilities – Active vulnerabilities rated Critical.
These KPIs provide an immediate snapshot of enterprise cyber risk health.

**🎨 Design Approach**
The dashboard follows enterprise reporting standards:
Clean executive layout
Blue shaded color coding 
Consistent severity hierarchy
Interactive slicers (Regulation, Business Unit, Severity, Control Criticality)
Clear visual storytelling
The design ensures complex risk data is presented in a structured and easy-to-interpret format.

**🧠 Insights Enabled**
This dashboard enables:
Identification of business units with concentrated cyber exposure
Monitoring of incident trends over time
Detection of regulatory compliance gaps
Prioritisation of remediation efforts
Assessment of overall control effectiveness
It bridges operational cybersecurity data with executive-level risk reporting.
